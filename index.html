Immersive Narrative

Artisan Heritage & Craft

"Every creation returned by the fire carries the soul of the soil and the heartbeat of its sculptor."

UrbanMitti was founded to bring the timeless wisdom of traditional Indian pottery back into contemporary corporate and private lifestyles. We replace toxic synthetic plastic, metals, and chemical containers with raw earth elements spun proudly by traditional Indian potters.

Our clay bodies are carefully dug from natural alluvial banks in Rajasthan and Gujarat, hand-spun on traditional wheels, dried organically under the morning sun, and wood-fired in clay kilns at optimal high-end temperatures.
